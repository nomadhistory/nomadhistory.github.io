# Story Check MVP

This is the first testable version of Historia Nomade's narrative diagnostic.

## Product goal

The tool is not a generic digital-marketing scorecard. It first tries to understand the real business — origin, personality, differentiators, people and story assets — and only then compares that identity with the self-reported digital version.

Core idea:

**real business story - digital translation = Storytelling Gap**

## Flow

1. Type of place
2. Why it began
3. Time / history
4. What guests remember
5. Personality (up to three traits)
6. Existing story assets
7. Mid-flow narrative profile checkpoint
8. Current digital channels
9. What the digital presence mostly shows
10. How clearly the difference is understood
11. How closely online matches the real place
12. Visible trust / social proof
13. Result

If the respondent has almost no digital presence, the digital-detail questions are skipped and the result reflects that absence directly.

## Result

The result includes:

- primary + secondary narrative profile
- Identity score
- Story available score
- Story visible online score
- Digital personality score
- Trust / social proof score
- Storytelling Gap
- strongest story signal to investigate
- description of what the current digital version communicates
- three tailored next opportunities

## Important MVP limitation

This version is self-reported. It deliberately says so in the result. A stronger next version should take public URLs and compare the owner's answers with what a first-time visitor can actually see on the website, Google, social media and booking channels.

No answers are sent or stored by this MVP; they remain in the browser unless the visitor explicitly opens the email follow-up action.

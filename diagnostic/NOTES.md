# Iteration notes

The current wording follows three rules:

1. Ask about memories, people and lived experience before asking about marketing.
2. Prefer concrete prompts (what guests mention, what people see in 60 seconds) over abstract brand terminology.
3. Do not overclaim. The MVP compares the owner's view of the real business with the owner's view of the digital presence; it does not pretend to have audited public channels.

## Questions to validate with real respondents

- Does the mid-flow profile feel insightful or gimmicky?
- Are 10–11 interactions short enough in practice?
- Does “Storytelling Gap” make immediate sense without explanation?
- Do owners distinguish “what people love” from “what we want to be known for”?
- Which story-asset options are frequently missed and need a follow-up branch?
- Should the next version request URLs before or after showing the self-diagnosis?

These observations should drive the next decision-tree revision before adding more scoring dimensions.
